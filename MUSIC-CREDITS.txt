Sternensturm – original adaptive battle score
Composition, arrangement and in-game sequencing created for Sterne.

Edits: selected orchestral recordings, peak normalization, limited tails, stereo 32 kHz AAC conversion.
The melody and adaptive arrangement are original; this is not a copied film or game soundtrack.

Cosmic suite for all five games (v92):
Tippen: Sternenschmiede — driving strings, brass responses and deep percussion.
Ausweichen: Durch den Schatten — tense low strings, pulse and rising orchestral layers.
Sternenschlange: Spur der Sterne — wider sustained strings and a soaring cosmic motif.
Supernova: Sonnenlanze — martial percussion, brass and fast string ostinatos.
Sternensturm: existing original adaptive battle theme, also used in cooperative play.
All motifs and arrangements are original. All games reuse the credited CC0 instrument recordings.
Sound effects: original Web Audio synthesis (crystal partials, filtered noise, bass impacts, spatial sweeps); no third-party effect recordings.
